2 Simulation of biochemical modem¶

Now we execute the model with the original parameters and the initial conditions. Let's start with simulating the biochemical model with a set of ordinary differential equations and then with the Gillespie's Stochastic Simulation Algorithm both available in the PySB package.

Deteriministic simulation¶

Using default values of ERK, AKT and FAK activity, representing high stiffness in the absence of erlotinib (i.e., DMSO (vehicle) control). Intial cycles are irregular and become consistent after several cell cycles.

Calculating a cost for this set of parameters¶

Once the simulation is done we can calculate a cost associated with the parameters. Let's try with our initial parameters. We have two conditions: high and low stiffness (55 and 0.6 kPa). We change the signaling input FAK, ERK and AKT as entries in the model to simulate these different conditions. Then the parameters of the fractional proliferation model are calculated and the difference between the intermitotic distribution for the low stiffness and the difference between the G1 and S to M time distribution for the high stiffness.
